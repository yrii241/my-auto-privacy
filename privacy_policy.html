Privacy Policy for My Auto

Last updated: 2026-02-09

My Auto is a personal car maintenance and fuel tracking app. This policy explains what data is used and how it is handled.

Data we use

  • Photos you choose or take inside the app (stored locally on your device).
  • Local app data such as cars, repairs, fuel entries, and notes (stored locally).

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal data on a server.
  • We do not share data with third parties.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you take a photo for a repair or car.
  • Photo library: used only when you pick a photo from your gallery.

Data retention

  • All data stays on your device unless you export it manually.
  • You can delete data inside the app at any time.
